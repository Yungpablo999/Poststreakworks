03 / VOICE STUDIO
The first paid creation feature: fast, useful, repeatable
Voice Studio is not positioned as a generic TTS wrapper. It is the narration layer inside a creator workflow: script to voice to assets to
scheduled post to maintained streak.
MVP product workflow
Step Creator action
1. Script Paste a script, use a PostStreak prompt, or import a saved series template.
2. Voice Pick a curated narrator, preview a line, add approved pronunciation notes and save a Series Voice.
3. Generate Render MP3/WAV, store the project, re-download later and track minutes used in a transparent wallet.
4. Publish Attach the completed asset to a post, queue it, schedule it and receive streak credit only when the post goes live.

Raw gross margin means subscription price minus only generated TTS cost. It is not net profit. Payment processing, storage, bandwidth, retries,
