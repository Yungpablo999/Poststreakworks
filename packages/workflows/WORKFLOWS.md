## 1. Decisions confirmed this stage (founder answers, 2026-08-14)

- **Streak-qualifying actions expanded.** Overrides Creator Engine Strategy's explicit "publish-only" rule (see §2 below for the full reasoning). Mission completion and collaboration completion now advance the same streak counter a publish does.

## 2. The streak-scope decision, and what it overrides

Creator Engine Strategy states this as an explicit rule, not a suggestion: *"A streak increments only after a successful post event in the user's selected Africa/Lagos day. Generating audio, typing drafts or opening the app does not count."* Its retention-rule section goes further: *"Do not reward raw AI generations; that creates abuse without creating consistency."*

The founders chose to expand past this — mission completion and collaboration completion now count. Worth being precise about what that does and doesn't undo:

- It does **not** reopen the specific carve-out the source doc names — merely *generating* something (an AI draft, a voice preview) still doesn't qualify on its own, in any workflow in this stage. `voice-generation-journey.ts` and `mission-lifecycle.ts` both still require a genuine completion or publish event, not a generation event.
- It **does** mean a mission as light as doc3's "Recovery: choose one unfinished draft and move it forward" (§10) now carries the same streak weight as finishing and publishing a real post. That's a real gap in effort between qualifying actions that didn't exist under the stricter rule.
- Because a streak is a per-day boolean, this isn't a volume-farming risk (you can't inflate a streak by doing five things in one day) — the risk is specifically about *meaning*: whether "day 47" still signals the same thing it used to. Worth watching once real usage data exists, not blocking anything now.
